Who I am

About Me

I grew up in an agricultural family in Pakistan, where I saw early on that a good harvest depends on getting the right inputs to the right place at the right time. Today I'm doing my M.Sc. in International Supply Chain Management at Rotterdam Business School.

Before moving to the Netherlands, I coordinated container shipments for a transport company in Lahore and ran my own import business, ANB Traders, bringing US dietary supplements to Pakistan. I then managed procurement for our family's 32-acre citrus and sugarcane farm.

Now, at Platform Zero, I research climate tech startups, which fits my interest in sustainable supply chains and green shipping. Outside work, I enjoy reading books. Back home I started a book club and set up a small library for children who couldn't afford books.
